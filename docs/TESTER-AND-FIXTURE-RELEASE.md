# Tester access and fixture interface release

The shared tester credentials redeem an invitation after personal email verification. They do not sign several people into a shared identity. Profiles are tied to local user IDs; invitation reuse cannot renew an existing grant. Each new invitation grants seven days. Administrators can choose a week, fortnight, calendar month, three or six calendar months, permanent free access or no complimentary grant, and separately suspend/deactivate/activate an account. Existing paid subscriptions remain independent of complimentary grants; suspension blocks protected access regardless of subscription.

The server validates adult date of birth, WhatsApp format and state/LGA membership. Location dropdown data contains 37 state/FCT entries and 774 LGAs from [Open Admin Data Nigeria](https://github.com/open-admin-data/nigeria-administrative-divisions), with Makarfi and Isuikwuato spelling corrections. Retain the upstream attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms. These are administrative labels; do not claim a fresh government verification of every spelling.

Presence records are at most one write per five minutes per active account. They show a sampled route and last-seen time, not continuous monitoring or an exact online guarantee. Up to 20 queued client-reported actions are sent per batch; the server retains the latest 30 allowed actions per account. Actions cover slip additions/removals, ticket generation, rule saves and PDF export. Client reports are informational and are not trusted audit evidence. Admin access changes have an audit record. Private credentials, query strings and form contents are excluded from presence. Bulk sports data continues to use R2.

The home card fan uses actual published fixture projections, including the limited public preview. It no longer depends on the presence of approved positive-value bets. Cards do not relabel projections as validated profitable opportunities. The bet machine uses one target combined decimal price with a disclosed 5% upper tolerance, actual quotes, chosen sports/markets, minimum model probability and one option per fixture. Saved legacy rules use their old minimum total as the new target. Search remains bounded, and global optimality is not claimed.

PDF and sharing metadata include competition/country when supplied; old records and unavailable geography remain explicitly unavailable. The URL points to the signed-in betslip dashboard, not a public shared ticket. PWA icon filenames change while the manifest identity stays stable. Installed launchers may defer icon refresh; physical-device acceptance is separate.

Tennis remains disabled. An offline scoring engine is not a trained, calibrated end-to-end tennis integration. Provider coverage, training and settlement evidence and explicit owner approval are required before enabling it.
