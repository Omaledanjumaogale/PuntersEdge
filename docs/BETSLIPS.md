# Saved betslips

The working slip remains synchronized through `betslip:mine` and `betslip:save`. Saving a named history record creates a separate `saved_slips` document and never overwrites the working draft. Each account can retain up to 100 named slips of at most 30 selections.

Authenticated `/betslips` lists only the current user's saved records. Owners can edit name, stake, type and selections while at least one selected fixture is scheduled, postponed or live, and can delete their own record. Every create/edit checks fixtures against authoritative archived schedule state. A missing authoritative unfinished fixture prevents editing. Each finished or cancelled selection is retained unchanged and cannot be removed or altered, while other unfinished selections remain editable. The whole slip locks only when every selection is finished or cancelled. Kickoff and live status do not prevent editing a personal record. The backend repeats these checks regardless of the UI state. Adding an already-finished or cancelled fixture is rejected. Personal records do not place financial wagers and do not modify the separate immutable model-performance archive.

The ingest workflow calls `internal.betslip.resolveSavedFixtures({states})`. It scans only unresolved records, processes at most 100 slips per transaction, schedules continuation when necessary, and writes only changed data. Final scores and statuses are retained in each slip so they survive fixture archive cleanup. History overlays the latest authoritative fixture scores, including provider final-score corrections, and falls back to retained scores after archive cleanup. Corrections update stored completed legs while a slip still has unresolved selections; all-final records use the latest archived scores when viewed. Personal selection details and recorded odds remain immutable after that fixture finishes. Postponed games stay unresolved; cancelled games void selections. Unsupported markets remain ungraded instead of guessing a result. Regulation scores are required for hockey regulation markets, and preserved regulation scores are used for football settlement when supplied by the provider.

WhatsApp sharing formats a named slip with recorded odds, stake and available scores. The PDF is generated locally by a lazily loaded jsPDF module and downloaded using the slip name. Neither export places a wager or sends payment. PDFs use the standard embedded font; unsupported characters are substituted to keep the document valid.

Saved histories and authoritative fixture snapshots necessarily consume some database storage and reads/writes. The 100-slip account cap, unresolved-record index, score persistence and unchanged-write checks reduce this usage but cannot make it zero.

Focused tests cover user ownership, separate working-draft preservation, missing fixture rejection, live editing, preservation of individual finished legs and all-final locks, retained final scores after archive cleanup, unchanged-write suppression, market-line settlement, regulation semantics and share content.
